# Design

Light operational workspace for planners reading dense tables beside spreadsheet exports in daylight. Restrained blue accent (OKLCH hue 230), white content, cool neutral sidebar, dark readable text. One system sans family, tabular numeric figures, 14px body and 28px page titles. Small consistent radii, thin separators, no decorative gradients or shadows.

Navigation: Overview, Orders, Materials, Quality, Uploads. Chat occupies a resizable-by-layout right panel on wide screens and stacks on small screens. Filters and snapshot metadata remain visible. Cards are reserved for actual grouped metrics; details use tables and rows. Loading, empty, unavailable, validation-error and retry states are explicit. Native controls and accessible labels take precedence over custom interactions.
