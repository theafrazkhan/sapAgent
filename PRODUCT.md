# Product

## Register

product

## Users and purpose

Production planners and quality managers review SAP exports in a bright office, compare exceptions across orders, and investigate supporting records. The supplied TMC SmartFactory proposal is the functional and visual reference.

## Principles

Calm, precise, traceable. Show snapshot dates and missing data. Preserve units and identifiers. Keep upload, overview, investigation and chat close together. Never imply live SAP monitoring or confirmed root causes. Avoid decorative marketing sections and synthetic performance claims.

## Accessibility

Target WCAG AA contrast, keyboard-operable controls, visible focus, labelled forms, reduced motion and responsive tables. Severity always has text as well as color.
